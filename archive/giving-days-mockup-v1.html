About Giving Days

If we receive 350 new gifts from members of the Trinity community between now and midnight on December 4th, it will unlock $150,000 in additional support for the Trinity Fund!

The Trinity Fund is the school's philanthropic priority and impacts every student K–12. Your gift today gets us one step closer to unlocking this challenge and reaching our Giving Days goal.

Ways to give
  • Online, right here on this page
  • Mail a check to the Trinity School Advancement Office, 139 West 91st Street, New York, NY 10024
  • Make a stock gift — visit trinityschoolnyc.org/support-trinity/making-a-stock-gift for information
How can we participate in Trinity Giving Days?

You can make your 2026–2027 Trinity Fund gift any of the following ways:

  • Online at giving.trinityschoolnyc.org
  • Mail a check to the Trinity School Advancement Office, 139 West 91st Street, New York, NY 10024
  • Make a stock gift by visiting trinityschoolnyc.org/support-trinity/making-a-stock-gift for information
Can we give before November 30th?

Yes, any gift made between now and midnight on December 4th counts.

If we normally make our Trinity Fund gift between January & June, how can we participate in Trinity Giving Days?

You can make a pledge during Trinity Giving Days, which can be fulfilled by June 30, 2027. By making your pledge now, your gift will count toward all challenges and our overall goal.

How are matches and challenges different?

Providing a match or a challenge is a great way to increase your impact. The difference between the two is:

MatchGenerates matching funds right away as donors or dollars come in. For example, John will match $100 per donor from the Class of 2000 up to $5,000, or Sarah will match all gifts from current parents up to $5,000.
ChallengeUnlocks your gift when a certain threshold is met. For example, Ken will give $10,000 if 20 alums from the Class of 1995 make a gift, or Samantha will give $10,000 if $100,000 is donated from all donors.
We already made our 2026–2027 Trinity Fund gift or pledge. Can we still participate?

Yes, any additional gift you make would count toward our challenge goal if you wish to participate. Please know how grateful we are for all your support of Trinity School.

How can we find out if we have already made our 2026–2027 Trinity Fund gift?

Please email trinityfund@trinityschoolnyc.org or call (212) 932-6860 and a member of the Advancement Office staff can check for you.
